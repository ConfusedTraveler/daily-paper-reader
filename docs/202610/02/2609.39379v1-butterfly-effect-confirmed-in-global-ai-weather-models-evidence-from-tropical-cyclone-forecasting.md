## 摘要
人工智能（AI）天气预报研究中最近出现了一个悖论。尽管有人声称AI天气模型无法模拟大气蝴蝶效应，但这与AI模型有限的可预报性以及AI集合预报的进展相矛盾。本研究通过反例证明，蝴蝶效应在AI天气预报中确实存在。对于超强台风“卡努”，AI预报受一个双吸引子系统约束。局限于两个区域的微小初始扰动触发两个局部吸引子之间的状态转换，导致第7天预测的风暴位置相差1006公里。这种行为与数值天气预报模型一致，并在过去5年中约12%的热带气旋中被观测到。这些发现验证了AI捕捉大气混沌的能力，并为AI集合预报提供了物理基础。

## Abstract
A paradox recently emerged in artificial intelligence (AI) weather prediction research. While some claim AI weather models cannot simulate atmospheric butterfly effect, this conflicts with AI models' limited predictability and advances in AI ensemble forecasting. This study demonstrates via counterexamples that the butterfly effect does exist in AI weather predictions. For Super Typhoon Khanun, AI predictions are constrained by a double-attractor system. Minor initial perturbations confined to two regions trigger state transitions between two local attractors, causing a 1006-km difference in the predicted storm position on Day 7. This behavior is consistent with numerical weather prediction models and observed in ~12% of tropical cyclones in the past 5 years. These findings verify AI's ability to capture atmospheric chaos and provide the physical basis for AI ensemble forecasting.
