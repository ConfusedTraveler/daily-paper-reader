## 摘要
本文针对有限离散随机变量，提出了一种外熵及其扩展形式——α-Rényi外熵和α-Tsallis外熵的非参数估计方法。我们建立了插件估计量的几乎必然收敛速度和渐近正态性。通过全面的模拟研究验证了理论结果。这些发现为基于外熵的度量在实际应用中的使用提供了坚实基础，包括预测、风险评估和不确定性下的决策。

## Abstract
This paper proposes a nonparametric estimation procedure for extropy and its extensions, namely the α-Rényi and α-Tsallis extropies, for finite discrete random variables. We establish almost sure rates of convergence and asymptotic normality for the plug-in estimators. The theoretical results are validated through a comprehensive simulation study. The findings provide a solid foundation for the use of extropy-based measures in practical applications, including forecasting, risk assessment, and decision-making under uncertainty.
