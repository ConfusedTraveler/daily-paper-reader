## 摘要
公平评分函数在气象预报验证中有着悠久的历史，并且最近通过使用概率空间中的稳定公平误差（SEEPS）评分来评估数值和机器学习天气预报系统的降水预报而重新受到重视。本文通过公平评分函数所诱导的最优预测，对其进行了系统分析。我们引入了广义对角线评分，它定义了一类广泛的公平评分函数，包括（在等价意义下）SEEPS、Gerrity、Peirce、Barnston和对角线评分。在该框架内，我们表明最优单值和分类预测的特征在于预测累积分布函数与气候累积分布函数之间的交叉点。此外，我们表明广义对角线评分在评估预测分布时是适当的但不是严格适当的，并且对远离交叉点的实质性预测错误设定不敏感。该框架还产生了对分类结果的概率预测既适当又公平的评分规则。对于单值和分类预测，交叉点特征表明，即使底层预测分布不变，最优预测也可能随气候不同而变化。因此，在公平评分下，相同的预测分布可能导致显著不同的最优预测，这对评估此类评分在特定应用中的适用性具有重要意义。

## Abstract
Equitable scoring functions have a long history in meteorological forecast verification and have recently gained renewed prominence through the use of the Stable Equitable Error in Probability Space (SEEPS) score for evaluating precipitation forecasts from numerical and machine-learning weather prediction systems.   This paper provides a systematic analysis of equitable scoring functions through the optimal forecasts they induce. We introduce the generalized diagonal score, which defines a broad class of equitable scoring functions that includes, up to equivalence, the SEEPS, Gerrity, Peirce, Barnston and diagonal scores. Within this framework, we show that optimal single-valued and categorical forecasts are characterized by crossing points between the predictive and climatological cumulative distribution functions. Moreover, we show that the generalized diagonal score, when evaluating predictive distributions, is proper but not strictly proper, and is insensitive to substantial forecast misspecification away from crossing points. The framework also yields scoring rules that are both proper and equitable for probabilistic forecasts with categorical outcomes.   For single-valued and categorical forecasts, the crossing-point characterization shows that optimal forecasts can vary across climatologies even when the underlying predictive distribution is unchanged. Consequently, identical predictive distributions may lead to substantially different optimal forecasts under equitable scores, with important implications for assessing the suitability of such scores for any given application.
