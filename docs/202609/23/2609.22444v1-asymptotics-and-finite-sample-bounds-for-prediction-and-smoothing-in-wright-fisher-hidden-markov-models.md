## 摘要
我们研究隐马尔可夫模型中的预测与平滑问题，其中潜在信号由多类型 Wright-Fisher 扩散给出，观测为离散时间的分类变量；研究动机来自重复抽样的时间序列场景，包括按时间分箱的古 DNA 数据，其中在有限多个时间点上记录有噪声的频率计数。我们关注在亲本独立突变下可得到的精确贝叶斯预测分布和平滑分布，以及它们与重复时间点内抽样下大样本目标之间的关系。对于固定的采集时间网格和趋于无穷的时间点内样本量，我们证明精确的 Wright-Fisher 预测分布和平滑分布在全变差意义下收敛到相应的总体转移律与桥律，其频率为极限相邻频率。随后，我们推导出预测律的显式有限样本控制，以及边缘平滑器的相应有限样本界。最后，在检查时刻，我们证明联合条件分布集中在目标频率处，其活跃坐标渐近服从高斯分布，而真实频率为零的坐标以更快速率收敛到 Gamma 极限。我们的设定除时间点内抽样外，对跨检查时刻的相依性不施加任何限制。分析依赖于 Kingman 溯祖块计数过程的一个固定区间尾界，该结果本身也具有独立意义。

## Abstract
We study prediction and smoothing in hidden Markov models with a latent signal given by a multi-type Wright-Fisher diffusion and discrete-time categorical observations, motivated by repeated-sampling time-series settings, including temporally binned ancient-DNA data, in which noisy frequency counts are recorded at finitely many times. Our focus is on the exact Bayesian predictive and smoothing distributions available under parent-independent mutation, in relation to their large-sample targets under repeated within-time sampling. For a fixed collection-time grid and diverging within-time sample sizes, we show that the exact Wright-Fisher predictive and smoothing distributions converge in total variation to the corresponding population transition and bridge laws at the limiting neighboring frequencies. We then derive explicit finite-sample control for the predictive law and a corresponding finite-sample bound for the marginal smoother. Finally, at the inspection times, we show that the joint conditional law concentrates at the target frequencies and that its active coordinates are asymptotically Gaussian, while coordinates with zero true frequencies converge to Gamma limits at faster rates. Our regime imposes no restriction on dependence across inspection times beyond within-time sampling. The analysis rests on a fixed-interval tail bound for Kingman's coalescent block-counting process, which is of independent interest.
