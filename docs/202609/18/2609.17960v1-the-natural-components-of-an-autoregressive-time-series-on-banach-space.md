## 摘要
我们证明，Banach 空间上的自回归时间序列可被典范地等同于有限直积，其各因子是由特征线性束的每个谱点对应的谱投影在各自子空间上定义的自包含且自确定的自然分量。我们假设该束的预解式除谱点外处处解析，而这些谱点可以是极点或孤立本质奇点。每个自然分量均表示为一个前向、后向或外向流，该流由遥远过去、遥远未来或指定初始时刻的上游边界条件所固定——取决于相应的谱点是在单位圆外、单位圆内还是单位圆上。

## Abstract
We show that an autoregressive time series on Banach space can be canonically identified with a finite direct product of self-contained and self-determined natural components defined on separate subspaces by the spectral projections for each spectral point of the characteristic linear pencil. We assume the resolvent of the pencil is analytic everywhere except for the spectral points which may be poles or isolated essential singularities. Each natural component is represented as a forward, backward or outward flow fixed by an upstream boundary condition in the far distant past, the far distant future, or at a nominated initial time---depending on whether the corresponding spectral point is outside, inside, or on the unit circle.
