## 摘要
高频工业信息物理系统（CPS）以低于10秒的间隔连续传输传感器遥测数据。预测性维护与运行控制需要数小时的多时域预测。然而，在扩展预测时域（dt=5 s时H=2,160步）上部署机器学习模型会遭遇双时间尺度预测困境：短时域动力学动量与长时域日周期热力学平衡之间的权衡。自回归滚动预测会遭受误差累积漂移，而直接多输出投影器则表现出方差爆炸和噪声外推。在本工作中，我们证明自回归互信息在扩展提前期上呈指数衰减至零，且最优最小方差估计量渐近收敛到周期性日基线（引理1与定理1）。受这些界限引导，我们提出动态渐近分解（DAD）框架。该架构将连续S形主导权转移调度α(h)与单调L2正则化缩放λ(h)耦合，将预测主导权从动力学自回归转移到日周期平衡，同时强制物理非负性。在30天工业遥测流（N=518,400步）上通过16个前向滚动测试窗口进行评估，DAD的总体平均绝对误差（MAE）为0.1764，相较于Deep LSTM（0.8143）误差降低78.3%，相较于PatchTST（0.4431）降低60.2%，相较于DLinear（0.2227）降低20.8%。计算性能分析表明，其在单CPU核心上以0.72 ms、0.203 MFLOPs和72k参数实现边缘执行（比Transformer基线快3400倍以上），并通过自动化双阶段CI/CD质量保证测试框架与物理故障安全架构得到验证。

## Abstract
High-frequency industrial cyber-physical systems (CPS) stream continuous sensor telemetry at sub-10-second intervals. Multi-horizon forecasting over hours is required for predictive maintenance and operational control. However, deploying machine learning models over extended horizons (H = 2,160 steps at dt = 5 s) encounters the Dual-Timescale Forecasting Dilemma: the trade-off between short-horizon kinetic momentum and long-horizon diurnal thermodynamic equilibrium. Autoregressive rollouts suffer from compounding error drift, while direct multi-output projectors exhibit variance explosion and noise extrapolation. In this work, we prove that autoregressive mutual information decays exponentially toward zero over extended lead times, and that the optimal minimum-variance estimator converges asymptotically to the periodic diurnal baseline (Lemma 1 and Theorem 1). Guided by these bounds, we propose the Dynamic Asymptotic Decomposition (DAD) framework. The architecture couples a continuous sigmoidal authority transition schedule \(α(h)\) with monotonic L2 regularization scaling \(λ(h)\), transferring prediction authority from kinetic autoregression to diurnal equilibrium while enforcing physical non-negativity. Evaluated across a 30-day industrial telemetry stream (N = 518,400 steps) over 16 walk-forward test windows, DAD achieves an overall Mean Absolute Error (MAE) of 0.1764, representing a 78.3% error reduction over Deep LSTM (0.8143), 60.2% over PatchTST (0.4431), and 20.8% over DLinear (0.2227). Computational profiling demonstrates edge execution in 0.72 ms with 0.203 MFLOPs and 72k parameters on a single CPU core (over 3400x faster than Transformer baselines), validated by an automated dual-stage CI/CD quality assurance harness and physical fail-safe architecture.
